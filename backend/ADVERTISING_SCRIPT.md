## Production Notes

### Visual Style
- Bright, youthful colors
- Real university campus locations
- Diverse representation (different genders, ethnicities)
- Authentic student expressions (not stock photo smiles)

### Audio
- Upbeat Vietnamese music or licensed tracks
- Clear voice-over with friendly tone
- Sound effects for emphasis (notifications, money)

### Text Overlay
- Large, readable fonts
- Vietnamese text with proper diacritics
- Price highlights in bold

### Call to Action Placement
- Clear CTA at end
- App store badges visible
- URL prominently displayed

---

## Ad Variations by Platform

### YouTube Pre-roll (15 seconds)
- Focus on problem/solution
- Strong hook in first 3 seconds
- Skip CTA visible

### TikTok (15-60 seconds)
- Vertical format
- Faster pace
- Trendy music
- Duet-friendly

### Facebook Feed (30 seconds)
- Square or vertical format
- Include captions
- Strong visual hook

### Instagram Stories (15 seconds)
- Swipe-up CTA
- Sticker elements
- Interactive poll options

## A/B Testing Recommendations

| Element | Variant A | Variant B |

| Music | Upbeat | Emotional |
| CTA | App download | Website visit |
| Price highlight | 70% off | From 50,000đ |
