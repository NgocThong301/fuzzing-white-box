## 1. Target Audience

### Primary Audience: University Students (18-25 years)

| Segment | Characteristics | Needs |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Freshmen | New to university, don't know book requirements | Course materials list, recommendations |
| Sophomores | Experienced, building collection | Better deals, sell old books |
| Juniors/Seniors | Specialized courses | Rare textbooks, competitive pricing |
| Graduate Students | Research-focused | Academic papers, expensive textbooks |

### Secondary Audience: Parents & Families

- Supporting children's education
- Budget-conscious
- Quality assurance important

### Tertiary Audience: Universities & Student Unions

- Bulk purchases
- Partnership opportunities

### Demographics (Vietnam)

```
Age: 18-25 years (primary)
Location: Major cities (Hanoi, HCMC, Da Nang)
Education: High school graduates, University students
Income: 3-10 million VND/month (allowance)
Tech Savvy: High (digital-native generation)
Online Behavior: Mobile-first, social media heavy
```

## 2. Customer Personas
